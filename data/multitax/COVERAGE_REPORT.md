# PA Multi-Tax Local Fiscal Panel — Coverage Report

_Source: DCED MunStats tax register (`data/pa_dced_raw/pa_taxes_YYYY.xls`), 2002–2019. Generated by `multitax_coverage_report.py`._

## Headline

**The spec's two hardest tasks (county-by-county millage for muni #2 and SD #3) are unnecessary.** The DCED tax register already used for EIT carries both municipal and school-district real-estate millage, with effective dates, in the same already-downloaded files. Every tax in the bundle (#1–#7) lives in this single source. County records are needed only to backfill a bounded set of SD-year millage gaps.

**Validated rule.** The summed total reproduces DCED's native TOTAL for **99.96%** of munis (one muni off by 0.4 mills — a genuine DCED rounding case). The critical step is deduping `(muni, tax_name)` before summing: the register repeats each muni's millage once per school district the muni spans, so a naive row-sum double-/triple-counts the ~57 multi-SD munis (e.g. Jeannette City summed to exactly 3× its true total). Blended-Rate rows are immaterial. This validates the summed total for 2002–2014, which lack a native TOTAL row.

The pre-existing panel covers only 2015–2021, carries ~59 duplicate muni-IDs per year, and its `re_total_mills` column matches the register's **General Purpose** levy for the median muni (corr ≈ 0.97, ~64% exact) but diverges by several mills in a ~5% tail where its value runs *higher* — i.e. it is an inconsistent mix of general-purpose and partial-total values, not a clean total. ## Panel scope & completeness (what is and isn't covered)

**Municipal and school-district levies: complete.** Every local tax these two governments levy is captured from the DCED register — EIT, property millage (GP + total), realty transfer, LST, business-privilege, per-capita, occupation (muni panel); RE millage, §679 per-capita, EIT (SD panel). SD millage gaps are backfilled from PDE to 99% coverage.

**County layer — one varying tax, not in the register.** The DCED tax register carries only *municipal* and *school-district* amounts (its `Total Tax Rate` = muni + SD exactly; there is no county field). The one county-level tax that varies across PA is **county real-estate millage**, set by the 67 county governments — it is NOT in this panel and would require a separate source (county assessment offices / STEB). Total property-tax burden on a parcel = muni millage + SD millage + (absent) county millage; the first two are here.

**Local sales tax — fixed statutory constants, no scraper.** PA authorizes a local sales tax in exactly two places: Allegheny County (1%, since 1994) and Philadelphia (1%, raised to 2% in Oct 2009). No other jurisdiction levies one, so it is hard-coded in `pa_local_sales_tax.csv` (see `multitax_sales_tax.py`) rather than scraped. This is why no sales tax appears among the register-scraped taxes above.
